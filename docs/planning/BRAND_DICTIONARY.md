# Brand dictionary and composition lookup (brand asked for -> composition -> what we have)

Status: analysed and decided 2026-11-09. **Phase 1 built and checked in the browser 2026-11-09 (uncommitted)** (see "Phase 1 as built"). Decisions below are the user's.

## Decisions

1. **The official CDCI (Common Drug Codes for India) brand master replaces the dataset workbook** the user first supplied (`Brand_Generic_Lookup.xlsx`, built from the public "Indian Medicine Dataset", scraped e-pharmacy listings, licence unclear). The workbook is used only to develop and test on a local scratch database. It is not put in the repository, shipped, or loaded into a real database.
2. **Dosage form becomes a product field first**, and matching takes it into account (a sustained-release tablet is not a plain tablet).
3. **Phase 1 is yes:** the reference tables, a re-runnable importer, the dosage-form field, and a "type the brand" search in Find alternatives with a dictionary fallback.

## The CDCI source (what is known, what is not)

- Published by NRCeS (MoHFW, C-DAC). The **Flat Files Package** is tab-separated text, released periodically (latest found: 31 Aug 2026), downloaded from https://www.nrces.in/services/national-releases under the terms in the package's `License.txt` (accepting them is the owner's step, not the app's).
- Files: Substance Master (3,283), Generic Master (10,305; generic medicines with reference to substance and drug form), **Brand Master (93,911; branded medicines with reference to the generic)**, Product Name Master (72,284), Supplier Master (8,213), Drug Form Master (423), Route Of Administration Master (160).
- **Checked against the real package (downloaded by the owner, release 31 Aug 2026):** tab-separated, `BrandMaster.txt` (Identifier, Brand Name, Product Identifier, Supplier Identifier, Generic Identifier, License Number, License Status, Excipient, last_updated_on), `GenericMaster.txt` (Identifier, Generic Name, Substance Identifier, Route of Administration, Dose Form, ...), `DrugFormMaster.txt` (Id, Dose Form), `SupplierMaster.txt` (Identifier, Supplier Name, Country), `ProductMaster.txt` (Identifier, Product Name), SubstanceMaster, RouteOfAdministrationMaster. A brand's "Brand Name" is "name strength form" ("Augmentin DUO 500 mg + 125 mg oral tablet"). A generic's name writes a combination with " and " and the salt in brackets ("Amoxicillin 500 mg and clavulanic acid (as clavulanate potassium) 125 mg oral tablet"), uses international names ("Acetaminophen") and "microgram". 93,911 brands, 93,897 linked to a generic; every license status is UNKNOWN (no discontinued flag).
- **Licence (`License.txt`):** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, with SNOMED CT affiliate terms. The package may be redistributed and built on **within India**; brand names belong to their owners. So the Find alternatives dialog shows the attribution, and the data stays in India (relevant if the app is ever hosted elsewhere).
- Coverage is smaller than the workbook (93,911 brands against 253,973) and it carries **no prices**. So the app shows no price from the dictionary; prices come from the shop's own stock.

## What the analysis found (workbook against the local prod copy, snapshot 2026-11-02)

- Only about 10% of the shop's 1,980 products match a dictionary brand by name (the shop mostly stocks trade-generic brands), so brand -> composition fills few missing compositions (47 of 193 Medicine products). The valuable direction is **composition -> what the shop has**: of 241,532 dictionary brands the shop does not carry, 31% have an in-stock same-composition product.
- 76% of the shop's compositions exist in the dictionary exactly. 112 salt names are unknown, about half typos or an "IP" suffix (`cetrizine`, `atorvastation`, `paracetamolip`).
- The existing alternatives rule ignored dosage form, so a request for a sustained-release brand matched a plain tablet. Fixed here by the product field and the form-aware rule.

## Design (phase 1)

- Migration 176: `product.dosage_form` (controlled list), `drug_ref_source` (one row per import: name, version, rows, active) and `drug_ref_brand` (brand, brand key, manufacturer, dosage form, composition text and key, discontinued, optional code and pack text; **shared reference data, no business id**).
- Importer (`api-v2/src/tools/import-drug-ref.ts`, `npm run import:drugref`): reads a canonical CSV (brand, manufacturer, dosage_form, composition, pack, discontinued, code) or a CDCI Flat Files folder (converted to the canonical rows), in one transaction; the new source becomes the active one and the previous is removed.
- Dosage form list (`dosage-form.ts`): tablet, tablet (sustained release), tablet (dispersible / chewable), capsule, capsule (sustained release), syrup / suspension, drops, injection, cream / ointment / gel, powder / sachet, inhaler / spray, other. `classifyForm` reads it from a drug-form text or a product title.
- Matching (`composition-match.ts`): same composition and strength; the form must be the same where both are known, a different known form is listed apart ("other forms"), an unknown form is included and marked.
- Find alternatives: below the existing product box, "Not in our list? Type the brand asked": searches the dictionary, shows its composition and form, then the shop's in-stock products with the same composition (and form), other strengths, other forms, and, when nothing is in stock, a few other brands of that composition to order. The existing Rx badge and "pharmacist's decision" wording stay.
- Dosage form for existing products: a manager action fills it from the product titles (preview first), and the product form has a field.

## Not in phase 1

Composition cleanup list (typos, "IP" suffix, fill from the dictionary after confirmation), the "asked but not stocked" log and weekly buy list, vendor availability and WhatsApp, a customer order. CDCI also offers generic codes (useful for ABDM), not used here.

## Phase 1 as built

- **Matching rule extended** (`api-v2/src/modules/app/sales/composition-match.ts`), used for both the shop's compositions and the dictionary: "(as ...)" brackets and salt words after the name (hydrochloride, sodium, calcium, sulphate, IP/BP/USP and so on) are dropped, " and " separates ingredients like "+", the international names map to the ones used here (acetaminophen = paracetamol, amoxycillin = amoxicillin, lignocaine = lidocaine, thyroxine = levothyroxine ...), "microgram" is "mcg". Different medicines, strengths and release forms still do not match. This also merges salt-form duplicates among the shop's own products, which is intended. Stored dictionary keys come from this rule: **re-run the importer if the rule changes.**
- **Migration 176** (+ rollback, re-runnable): `product.dosage_form`, `drug_ref_source`, `drug_ref_brand`; a test keeps the allowed forms in step with the code.
- **Importer** `npm run import:drugref` (after `nest build`): `--cdci-dir <folder>` or `--file <csv> --kind canonical|dev`, `--dry-run`, `--version`. It prints what it found first and replaces the previous list in one transaction. On the real files: 93,897 brands imported in under 7 seconds, 100% with a strength, dosage form recognised for all but 187.
- **API:** `GET /sale-alternatives/brands?q=` (a list loaded? brands starting with, then containing, what was typed; at least 3 letters or digits), `GET /sale-alternatives/brand?brandid=&storeid=` (composition and form from the dictionary; the shop's in-stock products with the same composition and form, a form not recorded marked "form not recorded", other strengths, other forms apart; when nothing is in stock, up to 8 other brands to order). The product lookup is form-aware too. `POST /products/dosage-forms/fill[?apply=1]` fills dosage forms from titles (preview first; never guesses, never overwrites).
- **Frontend:** a dosage form field on the product form; "Dosage forms" on the product list (preview, then fill); Find alternatives has "Not in our list? Type the brand asked for", "Same composition, different form", "Form not recorded" and "Other brands to order" sections, and the source attribution.
- **Checked:** 24-step API script on a scratch database with the real CDCI import (search, form, salt-form and spelling differences, sustained-release kept apart, order ideas, fill, permissions); 230 related backend tests and 34 frontend tests (7 for the dialog's logic); `ng build`. **Browser check (scratch DB, real CDCI data):** Dosage forms panel (preview), product form dosage-form field (value and 12 options), Find alternatives (brand search, pick, from-dictionary badge, other forms, brands to order, attribution, Add to bill). Found and fixed: a stray "No brand found" message after a pick. Known: with "All stores" selected (no store) the stock lookup finds nothing, as the existing alternatives lookup already did; the counter always has a store. The browser tool's mouse click did not reach Angular buttons, so clicks were sent through the page.
- **Not done / later:** the importer has not been run against a real database (only a scratch copy); CDCI has no prices or discontinued flags; composition cleanup list (typos such as `cetrizine`, filling missing compositions from the dictionary after confirmation), the "asked but not stocked" log and weekly buy list, vendor availability, a way for an owner to load a newer CDCI release from the screen instead of the command line.
