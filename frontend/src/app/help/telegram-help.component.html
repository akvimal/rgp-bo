Both use the one bot of the shop. Customers are not on Telegram. Only the Business Head does steps 1 and 2, once. Everyone who wants alerts does step 3.
1. Create the bot Business Head, once, about 2 minutes
1
On your phone or computer open Telegram and search for @BotFather (the one with the blue tick). Press Start.
2
Send /newbot.
3
BotFather asks for a name, which people see in their chats (for example RGP Alerts).
4
It then asks for a username. It must end in bot and not be taken, for example rgp_shop_alerts_bot.
5
BotFather replies with a token that looks like 123456789:AAHdqTcvCH1vGWJxfSeofSAs0K5PALDsaw. Copy all of it.
Keep the token private. Anyone who has it can send messages as your bot. Never post it in a chat or a screenshot. If it leaks, send /revoke to BotFather, pick the bot, and paste the new token into the app.
Optional: /setuserpic gives the bot a logo and /setdescription a line such as "Alerts from the shop". Leave /setprivacy as it is.
2. Add the bot in the app Business Head, once
1
Sign in as the Business Head and open Settings > Alerts. Open it now
2
In the Alerts on Telegram box press Add the bot.
3
Paste the token from BotFather.
4
App address (optional): the address people type to open the app, starting with https://, for example https://ops.myrgp.in. Messages use it to add a link to the right page. Leave it empty for messages without links. An http:// address is refused.
5
Press Check and save. The app asks Telegram whether the token is real. If it is, the bot's name appears in the box (Bot: @rgp_shop_alerts_bot) and the token is stored encrypted; only its last four characters are ever shown again. If it is not, you see the reason and nothing is saved.
6
Tick Send alerts to staff on Telegram in the same box. Alerts now go to every person who connects in step 3.
3. Connect your Telegram Everyone who wants alerts
A bot can only write to a person who pressed Start, so each person connects once, themselves. You need the Telegram app on your phone and to be signed in to it.
1
In the app open the bell, then Notifications. Open it now
2
In Alerts on Telegram press Connect Telegram, then Open Telegram.
3
In the chat that opens press Start. The page changes to Connected within a few seconds. (The link works once and for 15 minutes.)
4
Choose the level you want (everything, warnings and up, or critical only) and your quiet hours, then press Save. Press Send a test to check it reaches you.
What you will receive
  • Only the alerts you are allowed to see in the app, for your stores.
  • At or above the level you chose. Critical alerts come at any hour. The others wait until your quiet hours end (22:00 to 07:00 India time unless you change it).
  • No more than 12 an hour below critical, so a burst does not flood your phone.
  • A message that fails to send is tried again after 1, 5, 30, 120 and 360 minutes.
  • Counts, amounts, store names and a link. Never customer names or prescription details.
Commands in the bot chat
/status shows your settings   /stop stops alerts (press Reconnect in the app to start again)   /start with no link explains how to connect.
4. Daily digest to a store group Optional, Business Head
1
Create a Telegram group for the store and add the people who should see the digest.
2
Add your bot to the group as a member.
3
In the group send /chatid. The bot replies with the group's chat ID, a number that usually starts with -.
4
Open Settings > Notifications, paste the chat ID against the store, keep the switch on, and press Send test. Open it now
A store with no chat ID, or switched off, gets no digest. The digest never includes cash or margin figures.

Changing the bot, a phone or a person
  • A new bot: press Add the bot again and paste the new token. Everyone must press Connect Telegram again, because chats belong to a bot. Remove from the app takes the saved bot away and alerts stop.
  • A new phone: install Telegram and sign in, then press Disconnect and Connect Telegram on your Notifications page.
  • Someone leaves: switch them off in Settings > Users. They stop receiving alerts as soon as their login is off.
  • A test or demo copy of the app: use a second bot from BotFather. Two copies of the app using the same token take each other's messages, and test alerts could reach real staff.
For whoever hosts the app: the bot can also be set in the server's environment (TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN, TELEGRAM_BOT_USERNAME, APP_BASE_URL), but a bot saved in the app wins. The server needs outgoing HTTPS to api.telegram.org; no public address or open port is needed for Telegram.
