For the counter: a customer cannot be charged more than the MRP printed on the pack in their hand. If an older pack shows a lower MRP than the one in the system, bill it at that MRP. A product is listed only when the price the customer pays changed; a price that went up and came back is not shown. Only products in stock are listed: bought in the last year, not expired, and with stock left.
