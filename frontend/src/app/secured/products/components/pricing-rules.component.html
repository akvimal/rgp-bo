Class limits

Each product belongs to a class from two things: how far its cost is below the MRP (its trade margin) and how much of the profit it earns (A, B or C). Lowest markup is the least a price may be over the cost (before GST). Deepest discount is the most a price may be below the MRP. A price must be at or above whichever of the two gives the higher price. Usual discount is only a starting point: it is used to suggest a first price for a product that has none (always kept between the lowest price and the legal ceiling).
