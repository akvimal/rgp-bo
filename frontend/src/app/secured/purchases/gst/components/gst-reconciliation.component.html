How GST reconciliation works

What it is for: the GST you pay on purchases (input tax credit, ITC) can be claimed only when your vendor has reported the invoice in their own return, so it shows in GSTR-2B on the GST portal. This page compares the invoices you received with the portal's list and tells you how much credit is safe to claim this month.

The routine, once a month
  1. Choose the month with the calendar. Do it once GSTR-2B for that month is available on the portal (it is made around the 14th of the next month).
  2. Clear the notices at the top. A red one stops invoices being matched (for example, no supplier GSTIN). Fix it first: Fill from vendor copies a vendor's GSTIN onto its invoices, or open Vendors and add the GSTIN. Only received invoices of the month with a supplier GSTIN are compared; drafts are not.
  3. Download the file from the GST portal (the JSON of GSTR-2B for the month) and press Import 2B. Import 2A is optional: use it to see a vendor's invoices that have not reached 2B yet. Importing again for the same month and kind replaces the earlier import.
  4. Run Match. Each of your invoices is looked up by the supplier's GSTIN, then by invoice number (spaces and symbols are ignored). The value may differ by up to ₹1 or 1%, and a date up to 3 days. Running it again is safe: rows you have already settled are kept.
  5. Work the list using the tabs (below), starting with Mismatch and Missing in 2B, which hold credit back.
  6. Lock Period when you are satisfied, usually after the return is filed. It records the month's claimable credit and the credit on hold, and marks each invoice with its final status.

The boxes at the top
ITC claimable
Matched plus Accepted. This is the figure the Monthly summary under GST uses against your output tax.
On hold
Mismatch plus Disputed: credit you cannot claim until it is settled.
At risk
The credit on rows that are Mismatch or Missing in 2B.
ITC by vendor
Which vendors hold your credit at risk, biggest first. Start your follow-up calls there.
Good to know
  • Accept, Dispute, Exclude and Carry Fwd only change the status of the row. Fill from vendor adds a missing GSTIN to invoices, Create Invoice only starts a draft, and Lock Period records each invoice's final status. Nothing here changes a payment or your stock.
  • Changing an invoice's GST period or GSTIN needs another Run Match before the figures follow.
  • The tolerances (₹1 or 1%, 3 days) are fixed. A real difference is shown as a mismatch.
  • This helps you decide; it is not tax advice. Confirm doubtful items with your CA before claiming.
