How the reorder quantity is worked out

In one line: what we expect to sell before the next delivery, plus a safety buffer, less what we already have or have on order — plus anything customers asked for. Every figure is in units, and you can change the final quantity before ordering.

1 · Pace
Sales in the last Days ÷ Days
= units sold per day
→
2 · Expected
per day × Season × Target days
= sales until the next delivery
→
3 · Trend Qty
Expected + Min − Stock − On order
= what the pattern says to buy
+
4 · Ad-hoc
Open customer / manual requests
not yet received
=
Final Qty
The box you can edit.
This is what goes on the order.
A worked example
Days 30, Target days 21, one product:
Sold in the last 30 days60
Per day (60 ÷ 30)2
In stock now → Stock Days = 25 ÷ 225 → 12.5
Expected = 2 × 1.00 (no season) × 2142
Min stock10
Trend Qty = 42 + 10 − 2527
Customer request not yet received5
Final Qty = 27 + 532
Min stock is a buffer on top of the target days, so a row can need ordering while stock is still above Min.
The columns, in plain words
Stock
What can still be sold: everything bought (free units too) less what was sold, plus approved adjustments, on batches that have not expired and do not expire within 30 days — the same as Avail on the Stock screen, for the store chosen above. It never shows below 0; a red means the records say it would be.
Min
The product's minimum stock setting.
Sales {{days}}d
Units sold in the last Days (completed bills only).
Stock Days
How many days the stock lasts at the current pace. Under 7 is "fast moving". "-" means nothing sold.
Season
×1.30 means this stretch of the year sold about 30% more than the days before it, in the last two years. "-" means no adjustment (not enough history).
Expected
Sales over the Target days. A product that did not sell recently uses the Slow days window instead.
Trend Qty
What the sales pattern says to buy. Anything already on an order is taken off, and shown on the product as On order or On draft.
Ad-hoc Qty
Open customer or manual requests for the product, less what has come in against them.
Vendor
The vendor a request names, else the last vendor this product was bought from. Choose one in the row if there is none.
