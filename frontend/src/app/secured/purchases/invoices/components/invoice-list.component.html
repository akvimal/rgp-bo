Line totals that are not qty × rate less discount

A line's total is its quantity times the rate, less the discount, before tax. These lines are stored differently (a discount not taken off, or a line edited by hand), which throws the invoice total off. Correcting sets each to qty × rate less discount; the invoice total and balance follow. Each change is recorded.
