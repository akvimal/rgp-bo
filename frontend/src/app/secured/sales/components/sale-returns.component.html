How returns work
A return starts from the bill, not from this page. This page is where returns are settled and followed up.
1. Start
Sales › Reports. Find the bill and click at the end of its row. Pick the items, quantity and reason.
Only completed bills can be returned, and never more than was sold.
→
2. Check
Some products are not taken back (for example refrigerated ones) and the system stops them. Others are inspected first.
→
3. Settle
Here, press Settle. Say how many go back on the shelf and how many are discarded, note what you found if asked, and choose the refund: store credit, transfer or cash.
A store may cap cash refunds; above the cap use store credit.
→
4. Done
Stock goes back up by what was accepted. Discarded items leave stock and show Discarded in the stock history. The refund is recorded against the bill.
Status here: To settle is waiting for step 3; Settled is finished. A return that is still “To settle” can be cancelled; a settled one cannot. The owner chooses what is inspected or not taken back under Settings › Store › Returns.
